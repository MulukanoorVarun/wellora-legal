Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026 • Effective Immediately

Thank you for choosing Wellora ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the Wellora mobile application on Android.

We believe your personal health and fitness journey belongs to you alone. Wellora is designed from the ground up as a privacy-first, on-device application.

1. Information We Collect

Wellora does not require an account, username, or phone number to function. We collect only the physical activity data necessary to track and display your daily walking metrics:

  • Step Count & Motion Sensor Data: We read the hardware step counter / step detector sensor present on your device to count steps, estimate distance walked (in km), and calculate estimated calories burned.
  • Android Permissions: Wellora requests the ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission strictly to detect walking movement while the app is in use or running in the background.
  • Local Storage: All daily records, goals, and sharing preferences are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database.

2. Google Drive Cloud Backup (Google API Disclosure)

Google Drive AppData Integration & Sensitive Scope Disclosure

Wellora offers an optional feature that allows users to create and restore encrypted backups of their activity history using their Google account. When you connect Google Drive, Wellora requests access exclusively to the following OAuth scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata

  • Restricted AppData Folder Only: Wellora reads and writes backup files strictly within its own hidden, isolated Application Data folder in your Google Drive.
  • Zero Access to Personal Files: Wellora has no permission or ability to read, view, edit, or delete your personal Google Drive files, photos, documents, or folders.
  • No Third-Party Transmission: Your backup files are transferred directly between your device and Google's official cloud storage. We do not maintain intermediate servers or transmit your data to third parties.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Wellora's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party SDKs

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal health and fitness information. We integrate with verified industry-standard SDKs:

  • Google Play In-App Billing: Processes optional subscriptions for Wellora Premium securely through Google Play. We do not store or process your payment details.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to support free features for non-subscribers. AdMob serves non-personalized, family-safe advertisements in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your walking data is stored locally on your phone, deleting or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local health records. If you have stored backups in Google Drive, you can delete them at any time directly through the Google Drive backup management screen inside Wellora or via your Google Account Security settings.

6. Security

We implement standard encryption and isolated sandbox storage on Android. All network transfers for cloud backup are conducted over encrypted HTTPS connections directly to Google Cloud infrastructure.

7. Children's Privacy

Wellora is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
